HOW IT WORKS
The Universal Venue QR directs customers to NearbyVibes.com.
When scanned, Nearby Vibes combines the customer's GPS location with your venue's geofence to identify where they are and instantly displays your current live special.

Turn every visit into a future customer
When customers scan your QR code, they can immediately view your live special and are invited to create an account and enable notifications.
Customers who create an account are automatically enrolled in a 48-hour trial follow, allowing them to receive alerts whenever you publish new specials during that period.
If they engage with your venue or its posts during the trial, they automatically become an ongoing follower and continue receiving your future specials. If they don't engage, their trial follow simply expires after 48 hours.
Every QR scan is an opportunity to turn a walk-in customer into a returning customer.
