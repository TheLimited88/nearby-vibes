This Privacy Policy explains how Nearby Vibes ("NV", "we") collects, uses, and protects information when you use our Progressive Web App to discover Drink and Food special Posts from nearby venues, or manage a Venue account.

1. Information We Collect

  • Location data. Precise or approximate location, used to show live Drink and Food specials near you and to confirm venue check-ins (including QR scans and geofence matches).
  • Account data. Email and profile details you provide when creating a User or Venue account.
  • Usage data. Follows, post views, redemptions, and interactions with Drink or Food special Posts.
  • Age verification. A confirmation that you meet the legal drinking age in your jurisdiction, required only to view Drink specials — no government ID is collected.
  • Payment data. Subscription payments are processed by Paddle; NV does not store full card numbers.
  • Device and cookie data. See Section 5, Cookies.

2. How We Use Information

We use your information to show you relevant Drink and Food specials near your location, operate account features (follows, notifications, redemptions), provide venues with aggregated, anonymized analytics about their own posts, process subscription payments, and maintain the security and performance of the Platform.

Aggregated engagement data shared with venues never identifies you individually.

3. Your Rights — California Residents (CCPA/CPRA)

If you are a California resident, you have the right to know what personal information we collect, request deletion of your data, correct inaccurate information, and opt out of the sale or sharing of personal information. Nearby Vibes does not sell your personal information. You may exercise these rights at any time from My Account, or via our Contact form. We will not discriminate against you for exercising these rights.

4. Your Rights — EU / EEA / UK Users (GDPR)

If you are located in the EU, EEA, or UK, our legal basis for processing includes your consent (for location and notifications), performance of a contract (account features), and legitimate interest (aggregated analytics, security). You have the right to access, rectify, erase, restrict, or port your personal data, and to withdraw consent at any time without affecting prior lawful processing. You may lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority.

Where data is transferred outside the EEA, we rely on appropriate safeguards such as Standard Contractual Clauses. To exercise any of these rights, contact us via our Contact form.

5. Cookies and Similar Technologies

We use cookies and similar technologies for essential platform functionality (session, security), analytics (aggregated usage patterns), and preference storage (e.g., remembering age verification and location permission choices). On your first visit, a cookie banner lets you Accept, Reject, or Manage Preferences; you may change your choice at any time from the cookie settings link in the footer. Rejecting non-essential cookies does not affect your ability to browse Drink and Food specials, though some personalization may be reduced.

6. Age-Gated Content

Drink specials are age-gated: you confirm you meet the legal drinking age in your jurisdiction (21+ in the US, 18+ elsewhere) before Drink content is shown. Food specials require no age verification and are visible to all users. We do not knowingly collect personal information from individuals under the applicable legal drinking age in connection with Drink content.

7. Data Retention

We retain account and usage data for as long as your account is active, and as needed to comply with legal obligations, resolve disputes, and enforce our agreements. You may request deletion of your account and associated data at any time.

8. Data Sharing

We share data with service providers who help operate the Platform (e.g., Google Maps for location and venue data, Paddle for payment processing), and with venues in aggregated, anonymized form. We do not sell personal information to third parties.

9. Security

We use reasonable administrative, technical, and physical safeguards to protect your information. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

10. Children's Privacy

Nearby Vibes is not directed to children and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 (or the relevant minimum age in your jurisdiction).

11. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Material changes will be communicated via email or Platform notification. Continued use of the Platform after changes take effect constitutes acceptance.

12. Contact

Questions about this Privacy Policy or your data can be directed to us via our Contact form.
