About Us
Nearby Vibes is a New York City–based startup, built to connect great venues with people who are nearby and ready to explore.
We believe local hospitality thrives on spontaneity — last-minute plans, surprise specials, and neighborhood energy. Our mission is to make those moments easier to discover.
We're currently in MVP mode and working closely with our founding bars to shape the platform. As a small team, we value feedback, long-term relationships, and looking after the venues that support us early. Hit us up at hello@nearbyvibes.com.
This is just the beginning — and we're building it together, starting in New York.
For Users
Nearby Vibes is a real-time platform that shows you last-minute drink specials happening around you — right now.
No endless scrolling. No outdated posts. Just live, location-based deals from nearby venues that you can act on immediately.
3 Hot Tips for Nearby Vibes
1
Turn on Location Sharing to see what's happening near you in real time
To make this work properly, you'll need to share your location. That's it. We don't track you. We don't store your movements. We don't sell your data. Your location is only used in the moment — so we can show you the specials that are actually near you. Relevant, not creepy.
2
Enable Notifications so you never miss nearby specials or updates
Stay in the loop, and discover what's happening within walking distance. When a great special drops near you, you'll know. We hate spammy push notifications too — ours are smart, limited, and only sent when something good is happening near you. If it's not relevant, you won't hear about it. Simple.
3
Add Nearby Vibes to your home screen — it runs like a fast, app-like experience
Nearby Vibes is built to deliver a fast, app-like experience directly from your browser — install it to your mobile home screen and use it just like an app, without needing to download.
