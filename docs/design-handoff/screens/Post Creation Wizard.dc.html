Content Guidelines sheet
What kind of special?
Content Guidelines
Nearby Vibes showcases genuine venues and real offers. Keep posts accurate, honest, and compliant with food safety, liquor licensing, and advertising laws in your area.
Do
Post real, available offers with accurate pricing — drinks, food, events, and your venue atmosphere.
Don't
Misrepresent food or pricing, encourage excessive drinking, or post offensive, irrelevant, or spam content.
You're responsible for the accuracy of your posts and honouring promotions as advertised. Violations may result in content removal or account suspension.

"What is a Special?" definition sheet
What kind of special?
What is a Special?
A Special is a limited-time offer designed to bring customers through the door. Whether it's a discounted drink, meal, event or exclusive deal, specials create a reason to visit now. On Nearby Vibes, specials help venues attract more foot traffic, increase sales, and turn first-time visitors into returning customers.

Responsible advertising (drink)
Core fields
Responsible & Best Practices
For drink special posts · informational, not legal advice
Simple rule: if it feels responsible, welcoming, and adult-focused — you're on the right track.
Keep it 21+. Content and imagery must clearly target legal-age adults only — no youth-focused themes or visuals.
Right people, right look. Anyone shown drinking should appear 21+, ideally 25+.
No irresponsible drinking. No "all you can drink," intoxication, or drinking games.
No risky pairings. Never suggest drinking before/while driving, swimming, or sports.
Don't oversell potency. Share ABV factually — don't hook on "gets you drunk fast."
No "free alcohol." No free drinks, giveaways, or sampling — focus on value, not free alcohol.
Keep it respectful. No offensive, sexualized, or discriminatory content.
Promote the vibe: great atmosphere, quality drinks, social experiences, clear time-based offers. Think "come enjoy this," not "come overdo it."
