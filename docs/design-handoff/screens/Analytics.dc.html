Attendance Analytics Disclaimer
Attendance analytics are an estimate and should be used as a directional measure rather than an exact count of visitors.
Nearby Vibes uses browser and Progressive Web App (PWA) technology, eliminating the need for users to download a native app. As a result, attendance is only recorded when a user opens Nearby Vibes while within the venue's geofence. Customers who visit but do not open the platform during their visit will not be captured.
Attendance analytics are best used to identify trends, compare campaign performance, and measure the relative effectiveness of your specials over time, rather than as a precise measure of total venue attendance.
